### 1. Prohibited Misuse & AI Safeguards

Users and administrators are prohibited from using the Service to:

* Attempt prompt injection, jailbreaking, or adversarial input attacks designed to bypass safety guardrails or expose underlying system prompts.
* Reverse-engineer, decompile, or extract source code from Mhoo LLC agent orchestration workflows, custom APIs, or proprietary integration components.
* Submit Payment Card Industry (PCI) primary account numbers into prompt fields, protected health information (PHI) subject to HIPAA without an executed BAA, or classified government data.
* Attempt to disrupt Cloudflare edge routing, bypass Zero Trust access rules, or launch network attacks.

### 2. Enforcement & Reporting

Report security incidents or system abuse to **security@mhoo.com** or **abuse@mhoo.com**. Mhoo LLC reserves the right to suspend accounts immediately upon detecting AUP violations.
